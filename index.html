What the pretend teams are doing

Each one takes a player near the top of the published rankings, leaning a little toward positions it still has to fill, and a little toward whatever position just went twice in a row. Late on, it stops shopping and fills its empty slots — which is why every kicker goes at the end.

This is not a prediction. It does not know your family, and it cannot know who has a good season. It copies the one habit every draft room really does have: taking players roughly in ranking order. Treat the result as practice, never as a forecast.
